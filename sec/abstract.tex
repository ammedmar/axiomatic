\begin{abstract}
	We show that any construction of cup-$i$ products on the singular cochains of spaces is isomorphic --not just homotopic-- to Steenrod's original construction if it is natural, non-zero, minimal, irreducible, and free.
	We show that the axioms are independent, and use them to prove that all \mbox{cup-$i$} constructions in the literature represent the same isomorphism class.
\end{abstract}
