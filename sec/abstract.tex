\begin{abstract}
	We show that any natural construction of cup-$i$ products on the normalized cochains of simplicial sets, parameterized by the minimal free resolution of the trivial \(\Ftwo[\Sym_2]\)-module, is isomorphic --not just homotopic-- to Steenrod's original construction if it is non-zero, irreducible, and free.
	We show that these three conditions are independent, and use them to prove that all \mbox{cup-$i$} constructions considered in this article represent the same isomorphism class.
\end{abstract}
